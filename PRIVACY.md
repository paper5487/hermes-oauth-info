# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-09

This application is a personal-use tool operated by a single individual (GitHub: paper5487).
It accesses only the Google account of its owner, using OAuth scopes the owner explicitly granted
(Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Contacts, Docs, Sheets).

- **Data collection:** The app does not collect data from any other users.
- **Storage:** OAuth tokens and any fetched data are stored locally on the owner's own computer only.
- **Sharing:** Data is not sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties. Content may be processed by the
  owner's chosen AI model provider solely to carry out tasks requested by the owner.
- **Retention and deletion:** The owner can revoke access at any time at
  https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete local tokens.
- **Contact:** Open an issue at https://github.com/paper5487/hermes-oauth-info/issues
